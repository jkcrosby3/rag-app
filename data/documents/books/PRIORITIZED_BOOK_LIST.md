## 🎯 PRIORITIZATION CRITERIA

### Books were prioritized based on:

1. **Veteran Names** ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐
   - Direct cross-referencing with pension files
   - Prisoner lists, rosters, participant names

2. **Battle Details** ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐
   - Specific engagements, dates, locations
   - Complements newspaper coverage

3. **Diversity & Inclusion** ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐
   - African American soldiers (underrepresented)
   - Loyalists (balanced perspective)
   - Women's contributions

4. **Unique Perspectives** ⭐⭐⭐⭐
   - Prisoners, spies, naval forces
   - Camp life, daily experiences

5. **Space Efficiency** ⭐⭐⭐⭐
   - Small-medium sized books prioritized
   - Single volumes over multi-volume sets

### De-prioritized:
- ❌ General US history (too broad)
- ❌ Political writings (less veteran-focused)
- ❌ Post-war content (outside scope)
- ❌ Multi-volume sets (space inefficient)
- ❌ Pre-war colonial history (outside scope)
